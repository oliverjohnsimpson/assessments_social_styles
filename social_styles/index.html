Before you begin

  • There are two sections of 15 items each: Assertiveness, then Responsiveness.
  • Each item is a pair of opposite words. Pick the number that sits closer to the word that describes you.
  • Answer every item. There are no right or wrong answers; describe yourself as you are, not as you would like to be.
  • When you finish, you can download your report. A copy is also kept by Pelai Collective, where only its administrator can access it. Your answers are not kept in this browser: refreshing the page starts the assessment again.
